# Composable-world qualification protocol

Status: **frozen before qualification execution**

## Question

Within the declared component vocabulary and compatibility domain, do registered worlds,
coverage-generated compositions and protocol-frozen non-reference compositions preserve the
same compilation, transaction, resource, observation, lifecycle and replay semantics?

## Independent units and coverage

- 64 registered task–world units.
- 1,786 complete reference recipes across public boundary and categorical coordinates.
- 52 coverage-generated compositions spanning eight component patterns.
- Eight non-reference reaction–distillation compositions fixed by generation order.
- 32 module probes, seven cross-module interface paths, seven invalid declarations and
  192 invalid-action probes.

Discrete axes use pairwise covering rows. Authored continuous domains use seeded Latin
hypercube samples. Ordered workflow targets cover critical cross-component operation pairs.
The design is a finite coverage qualification, not an exhaustive enumeration of chemical or
task space.

## Measurements and pass conditions

Each unit records normalized construction input, compiler diagnostics, public contract, typed
actions, transaction outcomes, state-integrity checks, resources, public observations,
termination and exact replay. Missing receipts, denominator drift, non-finite quantities,
unexpected transaction outcomes, undeclared private-field exposure or replay mismatch fail the
unit. Invalid declarations and actions pass only when they fail closed with their registered
diagnostic class.

Module fixtures and directionality checks qualify the authored software models within their
declared domains. They do not establish universal or real-laboratory chemical validity.
